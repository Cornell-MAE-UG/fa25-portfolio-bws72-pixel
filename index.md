## About Me

![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

I am a sophomore at Cornell University studying Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science, driven by a passion for designing, building, and refining solutions to complex problems. I am particularly interested in the design process and bringing together mechanical components and software while continually iterating and improving a design.

Through my coursework, I have developed a strong foundation in solid mechanics, stress-strain analysis, and thermodynamics, alongside programming skills that emphasize precision, testing, and iterative improvement. I enjoy approaching challenges analytically, identifying weaknesses in early designs, and refining them into effective, reliable solutions.

I am motivated by ambitious engineering challenges and I am eager to continue developing my skills through hands-on experience in advanced, real world projects.
